# First two weeks, step by step

Do not change everything on day one. Week 1 is **capture + the A4**, nothing else. Week 2 adds the Friday loop. Week 3 is a review where you cut what you did not use.

---

## Before you start (30–45 minutes, once)

- [ ] Buy **one clipboard, A4** and a **fine permanent marker** + a pencil. The pencil matters: you will edit the A4 all week.
- [ ] Print **6 copies of `templates/03-OPEN-SHEET-A4.pdf`** at A4 **landscape**, 100%, no "fit to page". Black and white is fine. One for now, five spare.
- [ ] Print **25 copies of `templates/04-KICKOFF-GRID-A4.pdf`** at A4 **portrait**, 100%. One per working day for five weeks.
- [ ] Open `templates/03-OPEN-SHEET-A4.html` and `templates/04-KICKOFF-GRID-A4.html` in a browser, paste the **20 names** in the "Names" box, click **Fill rows**, then **Print**. The names are saved in that browser, so next time it is one click. Adding `Name, Area` in the box also fills the role column.
- [ ] Put the printed A4 OPEN sheet on the clipboard and **on the desk where you sit**. Not in the notebook. It must be visible without opening anything.
- [ ] Phone: open Telegram. The bot is now the inbox (see [`07-telegram-setup.md`](07-telegram-setup.md), 10 minutes). Until it is set up, use **Saved Messages** and paste into the app by hand.
- [ ] Put the A5 notebook back on the desk with a fresh page. The A7 goes in a drawer — replace it with the phone.
- [ ] Read `02-LEGEND-AND-EXAMPLES.md` once. Do not memorise it; the sheet's footer repeats the marks.

Optional but useful: a photo folder or Telegram chat named `whiteboard` so board photos don't mix with tasks.

---

## Week 1 — capture and the sheet only

**No app changes this week. No new meetings. No new report format.**

### Monday
- [ ] 07:20 kick-off: photograph the whiteboard, send it to Telegram **with a one-line caption** (`wk47 mon — AIR/FIR ready, ICSS server ok`). On the kick-off grid, one line per person, only what is new.
- [ ] 07:25 move the `⇒`/`→`/`?`/`!` to the A4. Write the week's **3 targets**. Write the two anchors. **Start the clock: 5 minutes.**
- [ ] 16:30: app. Enter the new commitments as TODO/TASK with **owner and date**, close what closed, write **one 5-line LOG**. 20 minutes, then close the laptop.

### Tuesday–Thursday
- [ ] Same rhythm. The only new habit is: **anything said to you goes to Telegram within 30 seconds.**
- [ ] Wednesday 16:30: add the `?` lines waiting on SHI to the Thursday agenda.
- [ ] Thursday, after the SHI meeting: update the A4 before you leave the room. Decisions are not re-derivable.

### Friday
- [ ] 13:00, 15 minutes: mark ✓/✗ on the A4, write 3 lines (done / blocked / risk). Then do the report **the way you do it today** — this week we are not changing the report, only measuring it.
- [ ] Note the count: how many report bullets did you have to pull out of your head? Write it at the bottom of the sheet.

### Weekend
- [ ] Nothing. No log on Saturday or Sunday. **Do not backfill.**

**Week 1 success = the A4 was on the desk and you read it every morning.** Nothing else.

---

## Week 2 — add the follow-up loop and the Friday 15 minutes

- [ ] **Monday 07:25:** new A4. Only live lines cross. Take a photo of the old sheet before you let it go — that is your archive, not the notebook.
- [ ] **Daily 16:30, extra 2 minutes:** before closing the laptop, look at the A4's `Next check` column. Anything due today gets its Teams message / email / corridor question **now**. Note the outcome with `•`.
- [ ] **Wednesday:** SHI agenda from the `?` lines.
- [ ] **Friday 12:45:** close the week on the sheet first, then generate the AI weekly report. Compare it to your hand-written 3 lines: whatever the AI missed is a `•`/`!` you failed to capture. Say so out loud once a week.
- [ ] Count the reconstructed bullets again. Week 1 number vs Week 2 number.

---

## Week 3 — the review (30 minutes, alone, with the sheets)

Answer three questions on paper:

1. **Which marks did you actually use?** If `?` was never written, you are not recording waits — that is the leak. If `!` was never written, you are swallowing risks.
2. **Which column of the A4 was never filled?** That column is wrong or the whole block is. Fix the template, not your willpower.
3. **Which of your 20 people had a blank row all week?** Those are next week's walk-and-talks. If the answer is "I don't know", the grid is not being used at the kick-off.

Then trim: delete anything from the method you did not touch in 15 working days. A method you trimmed is a method you own.

---

## Recovery rules (they will be needed)

- **Missed a day:** write `gap` and start today. Never reconstruct.
- **Missed Monday:** rewrite the A4 on the day you return; the week starts when you say it starts.
- **A page fills up:** do not start a second page. Rewrite the live lines onto a fresh sheet — that is the design, not a workaround.
- **Leave / rotation:** photograph the A4, copy it into the handover note, name the owner of each open line. 10 minutes.
- **Kick-off cancelled:** the grid is not filled. Fine — the A4 still runs.
- **Field day with no desk time:** Telegram only; the 16:30 slot becomes 17:00. Do not skip the nudges; skip the day LOG if you must.

---

## What to deliberately ignore for now

- Telegram bot, inbox screen, follow-up view, new app fields. Not before Week 3.
- Tags, dashboards, KPIs. The report you already have is good.
- Making the leads log. Not this quarter. Their 5-line verbal update comes first.
- Perfect handwriting, colour coding, any second notebook.
